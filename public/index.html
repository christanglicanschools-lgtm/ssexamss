Install CACG Results

Keep your assessment workspace on your home screen.

  • Android: open this portal in Chrome, then choose Install app or Add to Home screen from the browser menu.
  • iPhone / iPad: open in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Select Open as Web App when offered.
  • PC: open in Chrome or Edge and choose the install icon beside the address bar.

Open the app online once to save its pages. Teachers can then enable offline work from their dashboard.
